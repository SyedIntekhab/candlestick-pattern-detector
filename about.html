About EdCircles

Our vision

A world where every teacher's expertise outlives their tenure.

Our mission

A marketplace built on one belief: the expertise is already in the room. EdCircles just gives it a place to go.

What we do

EdCircles connects teachers, students, schools, and consultants in one marketplace, so the knowledge already inside education stops leaking out of it.

For teachers

Career guidance, CPD, and coaching in The Staffroom, from consultants who have taught, examined, and hired themselves.

For students

Career counselling, subject coaching, and exam preparation in The Classroom, built around the individual student.

For schools

Whole-school training and resources in The Library, including our facilitated in-service CPD programmes.

For consultants

A verified place to offer real expertise. Every consultant is checked before they take a single booking, so the badge means something.

Three circles, one marketplace: pick your slice on the homepage and everything relevant is in one place.
