Professional learning your teachers build together.

A menu of professional development offerings: schools choose whichever fit their staff, their priorities, and their year. Whatever you choose, the EdCircles hallmarks hold. Your teachers' expertise is the starting point, structured discussion is the method, and the school keeps an artefact at the end.

The programmes

Pick the ones that fit your school. Every one runs as a 90-minute or half-day session and ends with something your staff take away.

01Whole staff

Leveraging AI in the Classroom

AI for the craft of teaching: differentiation, feedback, and question design, with teachers judging what is good enough for their own students.

02Whole staff

Leveraging Tech in the Classroom

Low-friction classroom tech routines that serve the pedagogy rather than perform it, chosen, tested, and adopted by the teachers themselves.

03All teachers

Lesson Planning

Plan the same lesson segment twice, with AI and without, judge both against shared criteria, and keep the prompts that actually worked.

04All staff, annual

Child Safeguarding

Scenario-based safeguarding aligned to current statutory guidance: recognising, responding, and recording, updated for digital-era risks.

06Leadership and HODs

Curriculum Design and Review

Auditing, mapping, and strengthening your curriculum: coherence across phases, alignment to board requirements, and documentation that stands up.

07Leadership teams

Accreditation and Affiliation Support

Our experts help you run a feasibility study, then plan and launch IB, IGCSE, CIS, and ISTA affiliations, from readiness audit through to evaluation visit.

08Classes 9 to 12

Improving Board Exam Results

Departments mine their own marking history to map where marks are lost, and leave with a recovery plan and a moderated marking consensus.

Reducing Teacher Workload with AI

Every teacher automates one dreaded task in the room. The hours saved that week make the argument, and no belief in AI is required.
