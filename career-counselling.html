Career Counselling for Students

A one-to-one session to help a student think through subject and pathway choices, university and career direction, and what to actually do next. It is a conversation, not a test, and it is built around the individual student rather than a generic checklist.

Meet your counsellor

Bhavna Dhanak

University Guidance Counsellor

Bhavna is a University Guidance Counsellor with over five years of experience guiding high school students through academic planning and university applications. She has supported students applying to universities in the USA, UK, Canada, Europe, Australia, and India, and holds a Bachelor's degree alongside professional training in career counselling. Her approach is student-centered and holistic: she starts from each student's strengths and interests, works closely with parents, and helps students make informed, confident decisions about their academic and professional futures.

What's covered

  • Discussing subject and stream choices, and how they line up with the student's interests and strengths
  • Exploring career paths that genuinely fit the student, not just the most popular options at their school
  • Practical guidance on next academic steps, including what to research and who else to talk to

This is guidance to help a student think more clearly, not a guarantee of any particular academic or career outcome.
